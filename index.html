Forecasts are estimates. Do not rely on this app for navigation, boating safety, or any decision where weather could put you at risk. Check official marine forecasts and local conditions before you go out.

Data sources
Weather, waves, currents and sea level: Open-Meteo.com.
US tide predictions: NOAA Tides and Currents.
Maps: OpenStreetMap contributors.
Tides outside the US are model estimates, not official tide tables.

Your data
Your spots, limits, settings, fishing log (with the position of each session and catch) and last forecast are saved on this device only. Place searches and spot coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to get forecasts.
