Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

The short version: there are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. The app does not collect your name, email, or contacts, and it does not sell or share personal data. Your settings stay on your device.

What the app does

Not Sure Fishing Helper shows tide, wind, wave, current, water temperature and rain forecasts for fishing spots you choose.

What is stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only. We never receive it.

  • Your saved spots (names and coordinates)
  • Your fishing log: the sessions you record, with times, catch counts, optional species, size and bait, the weather, tide and moon conditions at the time, and the position of each session and each catch. It never leaves your device unless you export it yourself
  • Your wind, rain, wave and current limits, your unit choice, and your chart display choices
  • The last forecast the app downloaded for each spot, so it can show it when you are offline
  • A copy of the app's own files, so it can open without a connection

Clearing the app's data in your phone or browser settings, or uninstalling the app, removes all of this, including your fishing log. Export your log first if you want to keep it.

What is sent to other services

To show forecasts, the app sends requests to third-party services. As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address.

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) receives the coordinates of the spot you are viewing, to return weather, wave, current and sea level data. It also receives the text you type when you search for a place.
  • OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) receives requests for the map area you are viewing, but only when you open the map picker.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) delivers the open-source Leaflet map library, but only when you open the map picker.
  • NOAA Tides and Currents (tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov) receives a tide station ID to return US tide predictions, and may receive a request for its list of stations.

These services have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Location

The app asks for your device location only to place your fishing sessions and catches on a map, or when you tap a location button to add a spot. When you start a session, and each time you log a catch, the app records your position (latitude, longitude and accuracy) in your fishing log. You can also place a session's pin by hand on the map. These positions are stored on your device only and are not sent to us or to any other service. You can refuse the permission and still use the app: sessions then have no position, and you can set one on the map yourself.

The map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap when you open a map, which reveals the map area you are viewing, not your exact position.

Hosting

The app is served from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, when the app's files are loaded. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Safety notice

Forecasts are estimates and can be wrong. Do not rely on this app for navigation, boating safety, or any decision where the weather could put you at risk. Check official marine forecasts and local conditions before you go out.
